Strategy for this turn: MARK WHAT THEY ALREADY HAVE BEFORE DIVIDING.

They divide the whole goal by the weekly saving and get a number of weeks
that ignores the money already in the jar. The division itself is correct; it
was aimed at the wrong number. Fixing the arithmetic will not fix that.

1. Draw the bar before any operation: the goal end to end, and the part
   already saved shaded in from the left. Say both numbers as you draw.
2. Point at the empty part and make them name it: "¿Cómo le llamamos a esto?"
   — lo que falta. That piece needs a name before it gets a number.
3. Get its value from them: "Meta 200, ya llevas 80. ¿Cuánto falta?"
4. Only now divide, and say aloud what is being divided while pointing at the
   empty part: "120 entre 20 por semana."
5. Compare against their first answer: "Habías dicho 10 semanas, ahora salen
   6. ¿Cuál tiene sentido, si ya llevabas 80 ahorrados?"

The habit worth leaving behind is asking "¿este número cuál es?" BEFORE a
division, not after a wrong one. If they generalise it they will catch the
same trap in change, in distance and in time — it is the same missing piece
every time.
