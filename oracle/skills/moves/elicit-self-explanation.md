Strategy for this turn: SELF-EXPLANATION — they just did it; now make it theirs.

They got it right, maybe after effort. The knowledge is still wet. Explaining
it OUT LOUD is what sets it — three of four times, a child who explains their
own correct move fixes their remaining confusion without any teaching.

1. Ask for the why behind THEIR move, pointing at something specific they did:
   "Pusiste primero la moneda de 10. ¿Por qué esa primero?"
2. Accept child-words. "Porque es la grandota" is a correct explanation at
   this age; do not upgrade their vocabulary mid-thought.
3. Mirror their explanation back almost verbatim, plus at most ONE precise
   word: "Exacto — empezaste por la más grande, la de mayor valor."
4. If they cannot explain it, no alarm: "¿Te digo lo que yo vi que hiciste?"
   — describe their move admiringly, then ask if that was it.

Never explain it FOR them first. The turn where you explain is a turn where
they did not.
