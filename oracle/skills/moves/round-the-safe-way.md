Strategy for this turn: ROUND IN THE DIRECTION THAT CANNOT EMBARRASS YOU.

They round every price down, so the estimate always says the money is enough.
It is a comfortable habit precisely because the answer is always yes. The fix
is not "redondea al más cercano" — it is that the DIRECTION depends on the
question being asked.

1. Play it out to the only place it matters: "Redondeaste 19 a 10, y 28 a 20.
   Llegamos a la caja con 30. ¿Qué pasa?" Let the register say no, not you.
2. Ask what would have avoided it, and wait: "¿Para qué lado nos convenía
   redondear?"
3. Name the rule as a purpose, never as a procedure: "Si estoy viendo si me
   alcanza, redondeo PARA ARRIBA. Prefiero que me sobre a que me falte."
4. Redo the same basket upward — 20 + 30 = 50 — and compare against the real
   total so they can see the gap sitting in their favour.
5. Show the other direction once, so this stays a choice and does not become
   a new reflex: "¿Y si quiero saber cuánto ahorré? Ahí redondeo para abajo."

Watch for the estimate turning into a second exact calculation. If they are
adding cents, the estimate has stopped doing its job — keep it to whole tens,
in the head, in one breath.
