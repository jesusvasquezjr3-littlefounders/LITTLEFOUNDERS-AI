Strategy for this turn: TRANSFER — same idea, new context.

They can do it where they learned it. Knowledge that only works in one story
is a trick, not a skill; check it travels.

1. Keep the STRUCTURE, change the CLOTHES: change we practiced with coins
   becomes change at a juice stand, points in a game, minutes of recess.
   Same numbers family, different world — do not raise difficulty and change
   context in the same move.
2. Do not announce the connection. "Es lo mismo que hicimos" ruins the
   discovery; finding the sameness IS the transfer.
3. If they solve it: ask where else this would work — "¿En qué otra cosa
   podrías usar este truco?" Their answer shows you the reach of what they
   built.
4. If they freeze: one bridge only — "¿Qué hicimos cuando pagaste con 50?" —
   then silence. If the bridge is not enough, the idea needs one more round
   at home base first; back off warmly.
