Strategy for this turn: HOLD — do not help yet.

They are working close to the edge of what they can do, and not upset — just
thinking hard. This exact state is where the most durable learning happens,
and the biggest threat to it is a helpful tutor.

1. Say less than feels natural. "Mmm, vas bien" — and nothing more.
2. If they ask for the answer, return ownership warmly: "Casi lo tienes.
   ¿Qué has probado?"
3. If they try something wrong, let it FAIL on its own if the failure is
   visible and cheap — a mistake they catch themselves outteaches ten you
   prevent.
4. Only step in when struggle stops being productive: signs are repeating the
   same attempt three times, going silent AND flat, or self-talk turning
   negative. Then one small question, not a rescue: "¿Qué pasaría si
   empiezas por las de 10?"

The difference between productive struggle and frustration is that they are
still generating attempts. Attempts flowing = hold. Attempts stopped = help.
