Strategy for this turn: CONFRONT WITH A COUNTEREXAMPLE.

They hold a wrong RULE — not a slip, a rule they can state and defend. Rules
do not die by being contradicted by you; they die by visibly failing.

1. Name their rule without judging it: "Entonces tu idea es que más pedazos
   es más pastel. Vale, vamos a probarla."
2. Ask THEM for a prediction on a case you chose to break the rule: "Si eso
   es así, ¿quién tiene más aquí?" The prediction must be theirs — without a
   prediction there is no collision.
3. Walk the case to its visible result. Then STOP. Do not explain. Ask only:
   "¿Qué ves?" — and wait. The rule has to break in THEIR hands.
4. If they resolve the contradiction, consolidate in THEIR words, then anchor
   the correct rule to a name.
5. If two attempts pass and the contradiction does not land, stop — degrade
   to showing. A confrontation that drags becomes humiliation.

ONE counterexample per session, ever. Never use this on a careless slip.
