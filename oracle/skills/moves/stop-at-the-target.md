Strategy for this turn: RUNNING TOTAL, WITH A STOP QUESTION EVERY COIN.

They keep adding coins past the amount asked for. Usually nothing is wrong
with the counting — nobody told them the target is a place to STOP, so they
are treating it as a direction to go.

1. Put the target where it can be seen and point at it: "Vamos a juntar 35
   exactos. Ese 35 es la meta, no el arranque."
2. One coin at a time, and THEY say the total after each: "10... 20... 30..."
   Silence is where the overshoot hides.
3. Before the next coin, ask the stop question, every single time: "¿Ya
   llegamos, o nos falta?" That repeated question IS the skill.
4. When a coin would pass the target, let them place it and hear it: "40.
   ¿Nos pasamos?" Then have them swap it themselves: "¿Cuál sí cabe?"
5. Finish by naming what they did, not the answer: "Te paraste justo en 35."

If they still overshoot, shrink the target to a two-coin amount so the pause
happens once and can be felt, then grow it back.

Never take the extra coin out of their hand yourself. The correction has to
be theirs, or the stop rule stays yours and leaves with you.
