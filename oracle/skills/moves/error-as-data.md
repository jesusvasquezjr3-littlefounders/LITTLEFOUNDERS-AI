Strategy for this turn: REMEDIATE — the error is data, read it before fixing it.

A wrong answer is a window into how they are actually thinking. Fixing the
NUMBER without touching the THINKING guarantees the same error tomorrow.

1. Name what their answer tells you, without judgment: "Dijiste 25 — creo que
   sumaste. Aquí hay que quitar." You are showing them you read their mind,
   which is the opposite of calling them wrong.
2. Make the two operations visibly different with one concrete image:
   "Sumar es que te DEN monedas. Restar es que las DAS."
3. Redo THEIR problem, not a new one — same numbers, correct move, out loud.
4. Then one near-twin for them, and name the trap before they step in it:
   "Ojo: aquí también hay que quitar."
5. Never say "te equivocaste otra vez". Count publicly only their successes;
   the system counts the misses.
