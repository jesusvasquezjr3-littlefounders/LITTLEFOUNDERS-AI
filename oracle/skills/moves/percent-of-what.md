Strategy for this turn: A PERCENT IS ALWAYS A PERCENT OF SOMETHING.

They read "10%" as ten pesos. The words "por ciento" have gone invisible and
what is left is a number that looks like money. The repair is to make the
ANSWER change while the percent stays the same.

1. Ask the same question about two very different amounts, in this order:
   "¿Cuánto es el 10% de 100 pesos?" y "¿Cuánto es el 10% de 1000 pesos?"
2. Let them answer both before you say anything, even if both answers are 10.
3. Work them out side by side and leave both results visible — 10 y 100 —
   then ask the question that does the work: "El diez no cambió. ¿Por qué sí
   cambió la respuesta?"
4. Hand them the missing half of the phrase and make them finish it out loud:
   "Diez por ciento... ¿de qué?" A percent with no "de qué" is unanswerable,
   and they should feel that gap.
5. Anchor the recipe: "Por ciento es por cada cien. Partes el dinero en cien
   pedacitos y tomas diez." Same recipe, different pile, different result.

If they are still at "10% son 10 pesos", ask for el 10% de 10 pesos. One peso
is small enough to break the rule visibly and cheap enough to be funny.
