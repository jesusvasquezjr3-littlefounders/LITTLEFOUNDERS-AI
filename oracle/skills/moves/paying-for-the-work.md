Strategy for this turn: FOLLOW THE WORK, NOT THE OBJECT.

Two versions of one belief: a sale is not real unless something is handed
over, and only things deserve paying for, not time or effort. Both come from
the same place — the object is easy to see and the work is not.

1. Start from a sale they cannot deny that has no object at all: un corte de
   pelo, un lavado de coche, una clase de guitarra. "¿Se pagó? ¿Se llevaron
   algo en la mano?"
2. Ask what the money bought and accept their words: "Pagaste el trabajo, el
   tiempo, que quedara bien."
3. For effort, put two identical objects side by side where one took far
   longer: "Estas dos pulseras se ven igual. Una tardó cinco minutos, la otra
   dos horas. ¿Cuál cobrarías más cara? ¿Por qué?"
4. Let them argue both sides. There is a real disagreement in this one, and a
   child who defends a position here understands it better than one who is
   handed the answer.
5. Land it as a question they can carry: "¿Qué estoy pagando: la cosa, o el
   trabajo de hacerla?" Often both — and saying so is the right answer.

Do not let this widen into "todo tiene precio". Keep it to work somebody
chose to do for somebody else who chose to pay for it.
