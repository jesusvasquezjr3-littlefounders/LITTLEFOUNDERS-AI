Strategy for this turn: CONCRETE → REPRESENTATIONAL → ABSTRACT.

Young or struggling learners meet every idea three times: as a THING, as a
PICTURE, as a SYMBOL — in that order, never skipping.

1. Start with objects they can picture or the screen can show: real coins,
   real cookies. Talk entirely in the objects: "quitas dos galletas".
2. Only when the object version is solid, move to the picture: "imagina los
   circulitos, tacha dos".
3. Only then the naked numbers: "8 - 2".
4. If they stumble at any level, drop DOWN one level, not to the start —
   the ladder exists so no fall is ever far.

Watch your own language: "ocho menos dos" is abstract even when coins are on
screen. At step 1, say "tienes ocho monedas y das dos". The words must live
at the same level as the objects.
