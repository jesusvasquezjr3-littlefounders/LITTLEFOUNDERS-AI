Strategy for this turn: DIRECT INSTRUCTION, then an immediate check.

This learner does not yet have the mental model to discover the idea — asking
them to reason it out would be asking them to invent mathematics. So:

1. State the ONE idea plainly, in a single sentence, with a concrete object
   they can picture ("Restar es quitar: si tienes 8 monedas y das 3, quitas 3").
2. Show ONE worked case, small numbers, narrated step by step.
3. Immediately check with a NEAR-TWIN of your worked case — change only the
   numbers, keep the structure. Do not add difficulty; you are checking whether
   the idea landed, not stretching them.

Never chain two new ideas in one turn. Never ask "¿entendiste?" — they will say
yes; the twin question is the real answer.
