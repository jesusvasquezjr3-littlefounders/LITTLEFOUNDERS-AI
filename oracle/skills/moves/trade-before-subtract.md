Strategy for this turn: DO THE TRADE WITH COINS BEFORE SUBTRACTING.

They take the smaller digit from the larger one in every column, whichever
way round it sits, because "de 3 no puedo quitar 7" is TRUE and nobody gave
them the move that makes it possible. This is not carelessness. It is a rule
that works most of the time, invented to survive the times it does not.

1. Agree with them first: "Tienes razón, de 3 no se pueden quitar 7." Never
   correct that sentence — it is the one true thing they said.
2. Put the money on the table: 4 pesos con 30 centavos. Ask "¿cómo le
   hacemos?" and wait for the idea of changing a coin. Give it only if
   nothing comes.
3. Do the trade out loud: "cambio un peso por cien centavos." Now it is
   3 pesos con 130 centavos — the same money in a different shape.
4. Have them confirm nothing was gained: "¿Tengo más dinero que antes? No.
   Nada más lo cambié." That check is what makes regrouping honest.
5. Only now subtract, and write the columns AFTER the coins have moved, so
   the crossed digit records a trade they already did.

Pick one word — "cambiar" or "prestar" — and keep it for the whole session.
Two names for one move is how this gets learned as magic instead of as an
exchange, and magic is what fails on the next problem.
