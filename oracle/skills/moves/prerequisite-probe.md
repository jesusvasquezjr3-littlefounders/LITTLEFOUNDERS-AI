Strategy for this turn: DIAGNOSTIC PROBE — find the hole under the failure.

They failed something we believed they had. The failure is probably not where
it appeared; it is standing on a missing floor below. Your job this turn is to
LOCATE, not to teach.

1. Ask ONE small question that isolates the suspected prerequisite, with
   friendly framing: "Rapidito, para acordarnos: si tienes 10 y quitas 3,
   ¿cuántas quedan?" — small numbers, no story, minimum reading.
2. Do NOT teach yet, even if the gap shows immediately. One probe, one clean
   observation. The system decides what to do with what you find.
3. Keep the tone light and blame-free: this is "vamos a revisar los
   cimientos", never "a ver si sabes". The learner must not feel demoted.
4. Whatever they answer, respond briefly and without a verdict on THEM:
   "¡Gracias, eso me ayuda a ayudarte!" — the probe is for you, not a test
   for them.
