Strategy for this turn: BIGGEST COIN THAT FITS, THEN FILL WHAT IS LEFT.

They try to build every amount out of one kind of coin and give up when it
does not divide evenly — "no se puede hacer 35 con monedas de 10." They are
right about the tens. What is missing is that a pile is allowed to MIX.

1. Confirm the true part first: "Con puros dieces no sale, es cierto." Then
   reframe: "Podemos usar monedas distintas en el mismo montón."
2. Ask the fitting question, never the dividing one: "¿Cuál es la moneda más
   grande que TODAVÍA cabe en 35?"
3. Take it and say what remains: "Ya llevamos 10, nos faltan 25." The
   leftover is the same problem again, one size smaller.
4. Repeat to zero, then let them notice it themselves: "Siempre es la misma
   pregunta, con un número más chiquito."
5. Ask for a SECOND way to build the same 35 with different coins. Two
   correct answers is the fastest cure for "sólo hay una forma".

Keep the coins on the table where they can be picked up. This move dies if
it becomes arithmetic in the head — the point they need to feel is the
leftover shrinking in their hands until there is nothing left to pay.
