Strategy for this turn: DRAW THE STAIRCASE, ONE STEP PER PERIOD.

They apply the growth once and stop, because "crece 10% al año" reads as one
event instead of as something that happens again each year. The arithmetic is
fine. The repetition is what is missing.

1. Name the periods first, empty: año 1, año 2, año 3. Three boxes before any
   number goes into any of them.
2. Fill box one together and say the whole sentence: "Empezamos con 100,
   creció 10, quedan 110."
3. Then ask the question that carries the entire idea: "Ahora empieza el año
   2. ¿Con cuánto empieza?" They must say 110, not 100. That one answer is
   the lesson.
4. Let them do year 2 alone and notice the growth got bigger while the
   percent did not: "Ganaste 11, no 10. ¿Por qué?"
5. Let them name it and keep THEIR name for it: "cada año crece sobre lo que
   ya había."

Three periods, small round numbers. The point is the pattern, not the total.
If they can predict whether year 3 grows by more or less than year 2 before
computing it, they have it.

Do not introduce a formula this turn. A formula lets them keep the one-time
picture and get right answers anyway, which hides the misconception instead
of removing it.
