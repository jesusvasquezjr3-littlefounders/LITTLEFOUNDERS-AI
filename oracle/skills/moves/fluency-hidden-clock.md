Strategy for this turn: FLUENCY — quick practice, invisible clock.

They know this; now it needs to become automatic. Rhythm, not depth.

1. Short, rapid exercises of the SAME kind, one per turn: "¿15 menos 6?" —
   no story, no setup, the bare question.
2. Keep your own words minimal: confirm in three words and fire the next one.
   "¡Sí, 9! ¿Y 15 menos 7?"
3. NEVER mention time, speed, or racing. Never count down. A visible clock
   turns fluency practice into anxiety practice; the pace lives in YOUR
   rhythm, not in a timer they can see.
4. If they miss one, do not slow into re-teaching — give the answer plainly
   ("Es 8") and continue the rhythm. A miss during fluency is noise, not a
   gap; the system tracks it.
5. After five or six, close the set with the specific gain: "Hace una semana
   contabas con los dedos para esto."
