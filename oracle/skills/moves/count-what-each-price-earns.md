Strategy for this turn: COUNT WHAT EACH PRICE ACTUALLY BRINGS IN.

"Le pongo 100 pesos al vaso y me hago rico." This is not greed, it is that
only one of the two numbers is being looked at. The price is visible; how
many people say yes is not. Make the second number visible and the answer
changes on its own.

1. Take their price seriously and run it: "Va, 100 pesos el vaso. ¿Cuántos
   crees que vendes en toda la tarde?" Let THEM pick the number.
2. Multiply it with them, no commentary: 1 × 100 = 100.
3. Now the other price, their number again: "¿Y a 10 pesos, cuántos vendes?"
   20 × 10 = 200. Keep both totals written where they stay visible.
4. Ask the question and then stop talking: "¿Cuál te dejó más?"
5. Name it in two numbers: "Lo que ganas es el precio POR cuántos vendes. Si
   subes uno y baja el otro, hay que hacer la cuenta."

If they insist people will pay 100, do not argue — ask what THEY would do
walking past that stand. Then let them try a third price of their own and
predict before computing. A price they chose and tested beats a rule you gave
them. Two or three prices, whole numbers, one sitting.
