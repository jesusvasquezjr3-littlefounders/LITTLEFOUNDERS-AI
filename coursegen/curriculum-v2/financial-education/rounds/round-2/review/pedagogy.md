# Round 2 review: PEDAGOGY AND AGE FIT

Reviewer angle: an experienced primary-school teacher who knows worked-example research. Scope: the 8 round-2 plans in `coursegen/curriculum-v2/financial-education/plans/`, compared in general terms with `plans-round1/`. Read-only review; no repo file was touched. Engine limits already documented in `docs/content/FORGE-V2-PILOT-ROUNDS.md` ("Engine limits found by the pilot") are not re-reported, except where a plan applies the documented workaround only partly (items 7, 8 and 9 below say so).

Replacement strings were checked by script against the Copy Budget of their market (es/pt 6-9 = 15 words, es/pt 10-12 = 25, en 6-9 = 12, en 10-12 = 20; at most 2 sentences; help, `met` and `not_yet` under 160 chars). Words use the gate's own counter, so "R$ 4" is 2 words and "$10" is 1.

Totals: 1 blocker, 12 major, 25 minor (several of the minor entries bundle pure-taste remarks and say so).

## Verdicts (one sentence per lesson)

- fe-69-01 count-coins (Dina, 6-9): examples-first in practice, with the best-chosen numbers and the truest feedback of the set, but the reason behind each move lives only in unrendered `narration.script`, so the child sees the what and never the why.
- fe-69-02 change-counting-up (Rho, 6-9): examples-first in practice and the cleanest fade of the eight (visible whys, a part-then-whole guided pair, then practice and transfer); only polish remains.
- fe-69-03 needs-and-wants (Liruf, 6-9, B.11): examples-first in practice with a calm episode that ends "La próxima vez pregunto: ¿necesidad o deseo?", but the transfer key rejects a correct sort and tells that child "Todavía no".
- fe-69-04 market-stall (Zara, 6-9): examples-first for the first idea (stall baskets), thinner for the second (compare), and the whys for the moves are narration-only.
- fe-1012-01 unit-price (Liruf, 10-12, B.11/B.26): examples-first except that the decimals and the spoilage case that practice grades are never shown, and the expressions of guided-02 leak a hidden result.
- fe-1012-02 plan-the-budget (Rho, 10-12): examples-first with excellent "Paso / Camino" step labels (a model for the course), but the drop criterion is shown as preference rather than coverage and four expressions leak hidden results.
- fe-1012-03 saving-plan (Zara, 10-12): examples-first with a clean word-labelled board (no leaks), but the demonstrated check differs from the graded one, four turns carry the same empty prompt, and Zara's try/expect/adjust voice is thin.
- fe-1012-04 suspicious-messages (Dina, 10-12, B.11): examples-first in tags only, because "do not reply" and the "this one is fine" case are never demonstrated although both are graded, and the es/pt episode contradicts the en one.

1. [blocker] fe-69-03 / transfer-01 / all — `rubric.accepted["item-rain-jacket"]` holds only `{bin-need, why-will-rain}`. A child who sorts the rain jacket as a need with the reason "No podemos pasar sin eso" (`why-cannot-go-without`, true and the lesson's own criterion, and the reason the water bottle and sandwich take) gets `review/reason`, which is not met, so F- "Todavía no" shows for a correct answer. The scorer returns `review/reason` when a correct bin is paired with a reason not accepted for that bin. This corrupts the post measure. FIX: `"item-rain-jacket":[{"bin":"bin-need","reason":"why-will-rain"},{"bin":"bin-need","reason":"why-cannot-go-without"}]`. Apply the same check to every other item in `accepted` (water-bottle and sandwich take only `why-cannot-go-without`; if the reason list also offers `why-will-rain`, decide the same way for them).
