## Decisions taken on the conservative default (owner questions)

1. **A declined Mentor-mastery offer is final.** The topic is never offered again and is completed by playing it (the W3A lane's stated default, "the first decision is final"). Alternative for the owner: re-offer after new Mentor evidence.
2. **"Not now" on the early chapter is not recorded.** It hides the question for the visit and the offer returns on the next one: opening early is optional and asking again costs the learner nothing (no nagging copy, no badge).
3. **L-13 is shown inside the course narrative, not in a second panel.** `ChildDecisionsPanel` (W2L.4, Core's `/decisions` route) stays built and previewable but is not mounted, so the Family console shows each choice once, beside the lesson it belongs to.
4. **Choices are shown in the lesson's language**, not translated for the Tutor (Core stores the snapshot the child saw).
