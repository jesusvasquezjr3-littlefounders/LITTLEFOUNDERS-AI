## Implementation and rationale

**Where things live.** Rebuilt surfaces: `frontend/src/rebuild/site/` (a new directory for Lane 1's public-site surfaces, beside `rebuild/identity/`): `blocks.tsx` (shared page blocks), `Landing.tsx`, `HowItWorks.tsx`, `Families.tsx`, `Faq.tsx` with `faqItems.ts`, `LegalDocumentPage.tsx` with `legalContent.ts`, `CookieConsent.tsx`, `RouteErrorScreen.tsx`, `site.css`, and their test. They import only the design system and `i18n` (the `check-product-spec` boundary). Route bridges in `routes/marketing/` (`siteActions.ts` and one thin file per page) hand the surfaces the session-aware calls to action, navigation and analytics goals. Copy: new groups in `i18n/<locale>/rebuild-site.json` (`site`, `landing`, `howItWorks`, `families`, `faq`, `legalPage`, `cookies`, `routeError`). The legal documents moved from `marketing.json` to `i18n/<locale>/legal.json` unchanged.

**Composition (03 §3, 02 §4.5).** Each page is a neutral page with coloured bands: a hero, then sections whose paddings vary (tight, default, loose, a soft well and exactly one full-colour band) so no three share one rhythm; split sections at 7:5 or 5:7 from an 840 px container, one reading column below; the hero splits 7:5 only when it has art, and its art moves above the copy below 400 px (02 §7 rule 9). The families steps are a staircase from 840 px. The only art is our own: the four Mentor avatars (renders of the real models, 07 §4, from the manifest through `MentorAvatar`) and the pocket icons beside the chore consequence. No stock photograph, no raster bust, no video (the legacy hero loop and the concept graph would have put three idle loops on one screen, 02 §9.4).

**Calls to action.** Accent is the one call to action (02 §4.2): at most the header's "Sign up", one in the hero and one at the close (the audit's limit is 3), one of them breathing. A visitor's "Start free" starts a guest session and opens onboarding (M1; no sign-up), a signed-in person (a guest included) gets "Continue" to the app; the button that was pressed shows "Starting…" and, on failure, the error beside itself only. The family pages offer a visitor the brand line "Become your child's Tutor" (06 §3.3 `brand`, a link to `/signup?intent=tutor`), a verified parent "Open your family", and anyone else signed in "Open app". The legacy page sent every signed-in non-parent to `/verify-parent`, children and teens included; the rebuilt page never does, because minor safeguards follow age (OD-3) and the app's navigation already offers verification to an adult who is not yet a Tutor (Lane 0). The acquisition goals (`guest_start`, `cta_signup_start`, `cta_secondary`) are reported exactly as before.

**Copy.** Every string was rewritten to the site budget (06 §3.2) in English and translated from the glossary (OD-11): coins (never money, never "LF Coins"), Tutor only for the verified parent, the Mentor for the AI, no em dash. The landing headline and its second line are the brand narrative's short form ("The money pattern in your family ends with you. We'll show you how. You have to show up.", `COSMIC_NARRATIVE.md` §3), marked `brand`; their es-MX and pt-BR translations are an owner question (below). Every FAQ answer keeps the fact it stated (S04, S07 and S08 wrote them against the code) and now fits 25 words and 2 sentences (31 words for es-MX and pt-BR); "free to stay" became "free to start. There is no paid plan today" (OD-5). Two claims were removed rather than kept unbacked: the invented family panel and the "only an age band and a first name reaches the AI model" line.

**FAQ.** Questions are listed once in `faqItems.ts` (the literal `{ id: "…", category: "…" }` lines the gates read) and rendered as disclosures with a topic filter; several answers may be open at once, and `/faq#deleteAccount` opens that answer on arrival (a support reply can link one answer). The disclosure is a new shared control, `Disclosure` (`rebuild/design/disclosure.tsx`, exported from the controls barrel): a heading whose button reports `aria-expanded` and the region it controls, the whole row a 56 px target, the summary budgeted as a heading rather than a three-word action. It was added to the design system because the recomposition contract forbids a raw `<button>` in a surface; this is a Lane 0 file (see "Shared files changed").

**Gates repointed, not weakened.** `check-account-deletion.mjs`, `check-social-governance.mjs` and their tests, the Block D registries (`block-d-retention.json`, `block-d-scope.json`: list `rebuild/site/faqItems.ts`, namespace `rebuild-site`), `block-d-controls.json` (the approval and share-destination controls now name the rebuilt keys, and the retired claims are held out of `rebuild-site` as well as `marketing`, with a new test), `block-d-research.json` (scans `rebuild-site` for claims of proof) and `verify-social-governance-faq.mjs` now read the rebuilt FAQ. Every check that held the legacy copy holds the rebuilt copy.

**Terms and Privacy (M5, M6).** The text is Legal's, rendered whole as `legal` copy (06 §3.3) with line breaks kept; `legalContent.ts` walks every paragraph a clause has and stops at the first gap, and a test asserts no paragraph is dropped. The reader can switch documents, jump to a clause from the contents (each clause keeps its legacy address, `#c3`, `#s2`), search the clauses (case- and accent-insensitive, with an empty state), and open the cookie preferences from the Privacy Notice. The contents sit beside the text at 1:2 from 840 px and stay in view. The legacy copy-link, print and back-to-top buttons were dropped: the clause address is the contents link, and the browser prints and scrolls on its own.

**Cookie choice (M8).** `components/CookieConsentBanner.tsx` is now a bridge around the rebuilt `rebuild/site/CookieConsent.tsx` and keeps its contract: the `lf_cc` consent cookie of `lib/visitor` (unset until the visitor decides; rejecting deletes the visitor id and the optional analytics cookies), the banner only for a visitor with no session on a public page once identity is restored, `openCookiePreferences()` from the site footer and the Privacy Notice for anyone, and `onDecision` so measurement starts or stops at once. The banner is a non-modal region docked at the bottom, above the page's docked bar, that never takes focus; "Reject optional" and "Accept optional" are the same control with the same look, neither pre-selected. The preferences are a modal sheet (focus trap, Escape, focus return) that explains the three kinds (necessary: always on; where you came from: optional; visit measurement: optional), states the current choice, says that the two optional kinds are decided together, and links the Privacy Notice. One decision covers both optional kinds because that is what the product does; separate switches would promise a control `lib/visitor` does not have (owner question 3).

**X1 and X2.** X1 (not found) was rebuilt by Lane 0 on the single-state screen; this lane verified it and changed nothing. X2: `components/RouteErrorBoundary.tsx` keeps its logic (the stale-chunk detection for every engine, the console log) and renders the rebuilt `RouteErrorFrame`, which finds out where it was mounted: inside a rebuilt shell's `<main>` it is that page's content (the shell keeps its one `<main>` and its navigation); on a full-screen layer (the lesson player, the Mentor stage) it is the single-state screen. "Reload" is a full reload (the only fix for a stale version), the way home is "My lessons" (Learn) or "Home"; the message is announced when it appears.

**SEO (W2S.1f).** `scripts/seo/build-seo.mjs` builds each prerendered page body from `rebuild-site.json` (the landing, how-it-works and families sections, and the full FAQ as a definition list, which crawlers read as content) and the navigation label from `rebuild-core.json`. `scripts/seo/site.mjs`: the landing `<h1>` is the rebuilt headline in all three locales; the elevator paragraph, the descriptions and the agent summaries say "Mentor" for the AI and drop "answers out loud" (a minor's voice is off by default); titles and subject lines lose their em dashes; `lastmod` moved to 26 September 2026 for the four pages whose content changed. `routes/marketing/__tests__/seo.test.ts` holds each indexable page's prerendered `<h1>` to the rebuilt page's own, in every locale, and refuses "always free", "AI Tutor" and em dashes in any title or description. `seo:check` and `paths:check` pass; the routes and `MARKETING_PREFIXES` are unchanged.

**Legacy removed (this lane's own files, nothing else imported them, checked by grep):** `routes/marketing/{DecisionExercise,FamilyPanelPreview,MentorStrip,PrimaryCta,TechnologyGraph,TutorVisibilityDemo,LegalDocumentViewer}.tsx`, `alphaVideo.ts`, `{Landing,HowItWorks,Families,TechnologyGraph}.css` and the legacy `LegalPage` test. Kept for the S10 cutover: the legacy `marketing.json` strings (now unused except `badgeShare` by the retiring badge page), `common.json` `routeError`, and the `public/marketing/` media.

### Shared files changed (Lane 0-owned), minimally

1. `app-shell/PublicLayouts.tsx`: the site shell renders its outlet directly instead of inside `LegacyBody` (all six public pages are rebuilt; one line).
2. `rebuild/design/disclosure.tsx`, `disclosure.css` (new) and one export line in `rebuild/design/controls.tsx`: the `Disclosure` control (above).
3. `components/CookieConsentBanner.tsx` and `components/RouteErrorBoundary.tsx` (shared components, not in any lane's map): rewritten as bridges around the rebuilt M8 and X2 surfaces with the same exports and behaviour; `App.tsx` and `LazyRoute.tsx` are unchanged.
4. `__tests__/App.test.tsx`: the marketing-page cases now assert the rebuilt pages (the family-panel case went with the panel).
5. `scripts/audits/in-page.mjs` (the spacing rule of the proportion audit): targets in different floating layers are no longer compared. The rule already skipped pairs across the docked tab bar; it now reads the layer from the element (fixed or sticky position, `.lf-tabbar` or `[data-dock]`), so the sticky site header, a docked call to action and the cookie banner are measured among their own targets, never against the content scrolling beneath them (the scroll position, not the layout, produced those pairs). It only removes comparisons; no state gained a finding.
6. `scripts/audit-rebuild.mjs`: an opt-in `AUDIT_READY_MS` (default unchanged, 15 s) for how long a state may take to load. On this shared machine, under the other lanes' load, a cold page in a fresh isolated browser context took 30–100 s to boot (measured below), and the driver reported "never became ready" before measuring anything.
7. `scripts/verify-social-governance-faq.mjs` (S08's matrix of three FAQ answers): reads the rebuilt FAQ copy, sets the visitor's cookie choice first (the banner would cover the lower questions), and waits up to 90 s for a cold page.

## Decisions taken on the SPEC's conservative default (proposals for owner review)

1. **Landing headline.** The brand narrative's short form, verbatim in English, as the `<h1>` and lead (K36: the parent is the hero). The es-MX and pt-BR lines are AI translations of an approved brand line and are marked `brand`; the brand team should approve them (OD-11's recommended native review).
2. **Family-page call to action for a signed-in non-parent.** "Open app", never `/verify-parent` from the public site (the app's navigation offers verification to an adult who is not a Tutor). The owner may prefer an age-aware "Become a Tutor" here once the site can read the wallet classification Lane 0 uses.
3. **Cookie categories.** One decision covers "where you came from" and "visit measurement", because `lib/visitor` records one choice. Separate choices per kind (the M8 inventory lists them as two optional categories) would need a change to the consent cookie, the beacon and the analytics loader; proposed as a follow-up if the owner wants it.
4. **Removed marketing pieces.** The illustrative family panel (invented children and balances), the concept graph, the stock photographs, the video and the raster busts are not rebuilt. Replacement marketing art (a 4:5 hero scene) is an asset task under 07 for the owner's style review.
5. **Legal page tools.** Copy-link, print and back-to-top buttons are not rebuilt (the clause address, the browser's print and scrolling cover them).
6. **FAQ answers several open.** The legacy accordion allowed one open answer; the rebuilt FAQ lets several stay open and opens an answer from its address.

### Implementation and rationale

**Where things live.** Rebuilt surfaces in `frontend/src/rebuild/identity/` (this lane's directory in the ownership map): `authBlocks.tsx` (the heading block, the link to the other sign-in screen, Google, the password field, day/month/year, the form, the error line, the outcome frame), `SignInScreens.tsx` (A1, A2), `RecoveryScreens.tsx` (A3–A6), `VerifyParentScreen.tsx` (A7), `IdDocumentField.tsx` (the ID photo), `OnboardingFlow.tsx` (O1), `identity.css` and `identity.test.tsx`; `AgeScreen.tsx` and `KidSuspendedScreen.tsx` changed. They import only the design system and `i18n` (the `check-product-spec` boundary). The route files in `routes/auth/` and `routes/onboarding/` are now thin bridges that hold the session, the router, the API calls and the analytics events and pass state in (`useGoogleSignIn.ts` is their one shared hook). Copy: nine new groups in `i18n/<locale>/rebuild-site.json` (`authCommon`, `authLogin`, `authSignup`, `authForgot`, `authReset`, `authCallback`, `authUpgrade`, `authVerify`, `onboardingFlow`), budgeted in `rebuild/copy-budget/site.test.ts` on the app budget (onboarding on the 6–9 band, because a guest may be a child refused at sign-up).

**The sign-in shell (A1–A7).** Every sign-in screen is the content of Lane 0's `AuthShell` (the brand, one way back, one column that becomes a card from 640 px): one `<h1>`, the shell's one `<main>`, no card or glow of its own. `/upgrade-account` moved from its own layer into the sign-in routes (`siteAccountRoutes`), still behind `RequireAuth`. The one call to action is the accent button; other ways on are text links with a 48 px target, never a second call to action. A server error is one live error line where the form is; an outcome that replaces the form (sent, done, refused, verified) takes focus on its heading, while an outcome reached on arrival (an expired link, a failed Google return) moves nothing, so the skip link stays the first stop.

**A1 Log in.** One "Email or username" field (text, `autocomplete=username`, never `type=email`: a child's username is not an address, OD-3) with the help "Children use the username their parent made."; the password with show/hide; "Forgot password?"; "Create account". The specific reason for a failure (wrong details, email not confirmed, too many tries) comes from Core's code; anything unknown is "something went wrong on our side", never a raw code. Kept: the return to the page that asked for sign-in (with its state), `login_complete` with the analytics permission Core just resolved. New: an account Core has paused or removed (A.1: `/auth/me` answers `ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED` right after a successful sign-in and the session is dropped) now lands on `/account-suspended` instead of bouncing back to an empty form.

**A2 Sign up.** Name, email, password (8 or more, said as soon as it is broken; the reset and upgrade screens also say it before typing, the sign-up's first view has no room), date of birth as day, month and year with a real-calendar check ("We don't keep it", true since S01.2g stores only the age band), and "I'm a parent or guardian" as a checkbox (it records intent; `?intent=tutor` pre-ticks it; the account still starts universal and only verification makes a Tutor, A.5).
