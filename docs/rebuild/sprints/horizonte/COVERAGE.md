# Horizonte Visual: coverage of the 52 pieces

This table lists every Horizonte Visual piece (F0.1 to F4.9), the Core segment types it registers, its answer shape, its age scope, the catalogue entries it covers and an honest implementation status. It is read from the source, not from the plan: segment types come from the per-pack capability literals that Core spreads into its capabilities map (`HORIZONTE_CAPABILITIES`, the `*_CAPABILITIES` literals held in parity with the browser and Forge copies by `agent/tools/check-v2-lesson-capability-parity.mjs`), age scopes from each pack `contract.ts`, catalogue entries from the piece specs (`node C:/lf-wt/hz-specs/piece.js <id>`) and status from the lane docs in this folder, including each pack's "Fix round".

Written 2026-10-02 on branch `feat/horizonte-visual`, re-summed after the fix round (every pack doc's "Fix round" section and `forge-integration.md`'s). Counts in the tool snapshot below are produced by `node agent/tools/teaching-visual-coverage.mjs` and pinned by its `--check` and its test.

## How to read the status

- **implemented**: built in every layer the piece needs (Core model and scorer, browser board, Forge guidance and gate, three-locale copy, fixtures and tests) and covering the catalogue entries it claims, as far as the lane docs say. The note names every limit that is left; a limit is a boundary of what was built, not a missing layer.
- **partial**: built, but a layer of the piece, or a part it claims, is not built or not usable yet. The note names it.
- **missing**: nothing built. No piece is in this state.

Status count: 50 implemented, 2 partial (F0.4, F4.9), 0 missing. At the first merge it was 43, 9 and 0. The seven pieces that were partial because a catalogue entry was not built (F1.3, F1.6, F1.7, F2.8, F4.2, F4.5, F4.7) are now built, each with the limits named in its row.

**Implemented is not accepted and not released.** Every status below means "built and locally tested with focused checks". No piece has product acceptance, a requirement row closed, or a release. What has looked at a rendered board is thin and is listed in "Not verified" below: a headless Chrome render of the 19 `com` fixtures at 900 and 390 px, a seven-capture spot check of the `solids` nets, a 311 px probe of the ten `fin2` fixtures, and an Atlas audit (375 and 1280 px, light and dark) whose findings the lane fix rounds repaired from the code and which nobody re-ran. Everywhere else the 64 px hit size, text fit, drag on touch, reduced motion, spoken text and the declared chunk budgets are unmeasured: the harness runs in jsdom, which has no layout.

## Known gaps

Parts of a piece, or catalogue entries it claims, that the build does not cover:

- F4.9: the WebXR session has never run on a device; the age lookup needs the app to supply `learner`; there is no guardian consent record in Core.
- F4.5: no truncating interaction (m:E35) and no pouring of water (m:E33); the Archimedean solids appear only in the Euler mode.
- F4.7: no level-curve (contour) view (m:G19); no functions, chained powers or third variable in the typed formula.
- F4.2: nets exist only for the cube, the rectangular and triangular prism and the square pyramid.
- F2.8: tessellations are polyomino tiles on a square-cell floor, with slide, half turn and left-to-right flip only.
- F1.7: no tape measure and no half-unit or millimetre readings.

Engine and process gaps:

- F0.4: 26 of the 69 pack types register an F0.4 checker. The other 43 rely on the gate-4 function inside their own Forge pack file (a key-versus-payload proof only).
- The Forge behaviour space does not model the five seeded simulations (`math.chance-sim`, `math.galton-sim`, `stats.bootstrap-sim`, `stats.coverage-sim`, `money.life-sim`), so `forge-v2:check` on `emitted-horizonte.json` would fail on 15 segments and the file cannot join the shared `emitted.json`. The owner has to choose: a behaviour space that works without the learner's attempt, or a reviewed exemption.
- The staff lesson preview grades without an attempt, so a seeded segment is `invalid` there (fail closed, by design).
- Dark mode (read from the CSS: the constant `--ink` is `#11132a` in both themes, while `--surface` flips to `#131627`). Two packs still use it and were not fixed in this pass: `com` (`.lf-pascal-cell` and `.lf-bit` set their text `color` to `var(--ink)` on a `--surface` ground, so the numerals and bits are near-invisible in dark) and `alg1` (`.lf-alg-equals` fills the equals sign with `var(--ink)`, `.lf-alg-art path` strokes with it over `--sky-soft`, and `.lf-alg-tile--neg rect` outlines with it). The fix is `var(--content)`, as the other packs did. The other packs read flipping tokens by their lane docs (alg2, num-b, plane1, prob, stats1, sim1, sim2, solids, space1, space2 and the F1.0 charts were repaired; num-a and fin2 were audited and had no constant ink), and a search of the horizonte stylesheets finds no other `var(--ink)`. This is a source search, not a look at a render.
- The shared disabled `Move to`, `Reset` and `Check` buttons measure about 2.3:1 in light and 2.7:1 in dark in every pack's audit. They belong to the shared `segmentKit` and design layer, not to a pack.
