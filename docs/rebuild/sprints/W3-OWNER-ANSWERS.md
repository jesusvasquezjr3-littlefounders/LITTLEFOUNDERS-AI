## Decisions taken where the answers are silent (conservative defaults)

- **M-12.** A Tutor-dated tween may join C.17 with the guardian's analytics consent (OD-26 read as the specific rule over H-20). The generic exposure route records only for a self-managed adult whose analytics rule allows it.
- **S-03.** A linked guardian gets no active notice when a 16-17-year-old turns discoverable (the audit row and the guardian's existing full view only).
- **L-13.** A child with no birth date whose age screen said under 13 stays eligible until a birth date says otherwise (the band is a snapshot).
- **P-03.** Mastery is checked at confirmation and early access is kept if the Mentor's estimate later dips; the age conditions are re-checked on every read.
- **P-04.** An accepted Mentor-mastery completion does not earn the stage badge on its own (the reversible side; B4 badges are never revoked).
- **L-02.** A near miss (`almost`) keeps the tap until Product says otherwise.
