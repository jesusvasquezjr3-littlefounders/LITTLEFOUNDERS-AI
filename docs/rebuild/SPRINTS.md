# Migration sprints and acceptance register

The binding authority is [the LittleFounders SPEC](../littlefounders-spec/README.md). Sprints sequence its requirements; they do not reduce scope or replace acceptance criteria. All documentation and evidence are in English. The owner requested point-by-point implementation with product and frontend verification on 21 September 2026.

## Status contract

- **Planned:** scoped, no implementation evidence yet.
- **In progress:** implementation or required verification is incomplete.
- **Locally verified checkpoint:** the named, bounded change passes its recorded checks. This does not close the parent requirement or certify production.
- **Accepted:** all requirement criteria, applicable appendix Definition of Done, frontend checks, data checks and required human review are satisfied and linked.
- **Released:** accepted work has shipped and post-release checks are recorded.

A passing build, screenshot or unit test alone never means Accepted. No sprint is complete while its required criteria, migration verification or review remain outstanding. Record failed attempts and their resolution; never replace a failed result with an unsupported explanation.
