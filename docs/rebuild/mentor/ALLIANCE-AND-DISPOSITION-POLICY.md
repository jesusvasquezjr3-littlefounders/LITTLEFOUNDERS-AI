Written 25 September 2026 in S06 (the orchestrator's wave label S06.5; the sprint record numbers the work S06.8 for C.14 and S06.9 for C.15/C.7). ## 2. C.15 — the Alliance Controller (`oracle/src/tutor/allianceController.ts`)

A sibling of the pedagogy controller. It does not choose strategies; it tracks bond, goal agreement and task agreement as explicit session state and owns four moves.

- **Goal agreement (opening move).** On the learner's first substantive message the Mentor restates, in ONE question and in the learner's own words, what the session is for, connected to today's topic ("So today you want to figure out whether to save for the bike or spend now, right?"). The turn carries no activity and no adaptation offer. The server sends `goal_check`; the learner answers on two equal chips (`goal_response`, refused with `NO_GOAL_CHECK` unless open) or in words (`classifyGoalReply`). "Yes" settles it (`agreed`); "something else" makes the Mentor ask what they would like instead, offering two money choices, and the learner's answer becomes the goal (`renegotiated`); anything else is recorded `unconfirmed` and answered as an ordinary turn. The move never loops. A session that never reached a learner message is `not_reached`.
- **Task agreement (renegotiation trigger).** Every delivered adaptation offer, acceptance and decline is recorded. Two consecutive declines with no acceptance between them (proposed) is the defined pattern: the Mentor's next turn only reacts, and the SYSTEM asks the written question "That way isn't working. What would help you more right now?" (it names the method, never the learner). The learner answers in words and the Mentor follows it (or offers two concrete ways to continue). A renegotiation a turn could not carry stays due for the next turn; a Mentor turn that went out without it is counted, so an end of session with it still due is `undelivered` (a real miss). The C.19 check-in wins over it and supersedes it (the check-in is itself a renegotiation of the task). At most two per session.
- **Improvement window (Stage 7 input).** After a renegotiation is answered (or passed over by an activity), the next four learner turns are watched: a further decline, a disengagement firing or a "not really" at a check-in makes it `improved: false`; four clean turns make it `improved: true`; a session that ends first leaves it unknown.
- **Bond.** Every delivered Mentor turn's praise is classified by the C.18 reader: praise that names something the learner actually did (`specific`) is counted apart from generic praise. The end-of-session **bond proxy** ("Did I get what you wanted today?", three equal chips: yes / a little / not really) is answered on the closing surface after the session closes (`POST /api/v1/tutor/sessions/:id/alliance-check`): the learner's own session only (a verified Tutor never answers for the child), once, within 24 hours, never after a safety stop, closed values only.
- **Persona continuity.** Core decides, from the persistent persona-rapport history (C.7) merged with the learner's recent session rows, whether THIS persona has worked with this learner: `first_meeting`, `persona_switch`, `memory_gap` (more than 30 days) or `continuing`. For the first three the Mentor re-establishes bond, goal and task framing: a written, per-character introduction (`introduce`) or reconnection (`reconnect`) replaces a greeting that assumes a history (Liruf's "You came back!"), each ending with the goal question; every model turn of a first meeting or persona switch carries a fixed system sentence ("you have NOT worked with this learner before as yourself…"), and the memory-gap note rides the first three learner turns; a turn that claims a shared history ("last time we…", "I remember you", "good to see you again") is repaired once and never delivered (`claimsSharedHistory`). A queued C.16 re-engagement keeps precedence and its wording is now persona-neutral ("Your last session stopped partway").

## 3. C.14 — the self-explanation move (`oracle/src/tutor/selfExplanation.ts`)

- **Financial decision points.** A graded decision activity (`piggy_split`, `needs_wants`, `price_compare`, `budget_fit`, `savings_goal`, `fair_trade`, `best_decision`, `story_branch`, `dialogue_choice`, `would_you_rather`; counting and computing types are not decisions), or the learner's answer to the Mentor's own money-decision question (`isDecisionQuestion` on the Mentor's line, and `answersDecision` on the reply: "save it" is a choice, "lol" is not). An unsound stated decision belongs to C.18 and is not prompted.
- **The move.** The Mentor's reacting turn only reacts (no explanation, no question, no activity); the SYSTEM then asks a written, pre-generatable question: "Why did you pick that?" (`why`), "Tell me how you decided that." after a verified-wrong decision (`how`, asking for the thinking without framing the answer as a mistake to justify), or a sentence stem "Finish my sentence: I picked it because…" for a learner whose profile shows explanations rarely name the idea (`scaffolded`, C.7). At most four per session and at least three learner turns after the previous move ENDED (proposed), so it never becomes an interrogation.
- **The quality check** (`classifyExplanation`, Appendix D §3.3): does the reply name the idea the decision rests on (a closed family: saving, spending, needs vs wants, price and value, budget, earning, trade, sharing, time), or is it filler ("because it's right", "porque sí", "sei lá", three words or fewer naming nothing)? A pass is acknowledged SPECIFICALLY in the learner's own terms. A low-quality reply gets ONE targeted follow-up that points at the concept without giving the reason away. A second low-quality reply gets the reason stated once by the Mentor, and the lesson moves on. A stated wrong idea goes to C.18 (corrected, never praised; `misconception_corrected`); a help request goes to the C.13 hint ladder (`skipped_help`). The check answers "did they name the idea", not "is it correct": the lightweight check the SPEC asks for.
- **The record** (`tutor_self_explanation_event`): decision source, concept family, variant, the quality label of each reply and the outcome. Never what the learner said.

## 4. C.7 — the learner disposition profile

- **Where it lives.** `learner_disposition_profile` (Core writes it; RLS lets the learner and a verified guardian read it; no client write policy). Populated at every close by Core's `services/pedagogy/disposition.ts` from what C.9 (disengagement firings, check-in answers) and C.15 / C.14 (help requests, reply pace, adaptations taken or turned down, self-explanation passes, the persona and the bond proxy) observed: exponentially weighted rates, decayed counts, per-persona rapport. Closed-label traits: help style (`independent`, `hint_seeking`, `tell_early`), persistence (`persists`, `disengages_early`), explanation (`explains`, `needs_scaffold`), each `unknown` until three behavioural sessions (four decayed prompts for explanation).
- **How the controller reads it** (beside the mastery values, never instead of them). Core sends a closed projection in the SESSION context, only to an Oracle that announces it (`x-oracle-context-fields`). It is not a field of the sealed model context, so the 14-field privacy contract is untouched and nothing about a child's disposition reaches a model. Its effects, each bounded and reported at close (`disposition.applied`):
  - `stuck_degrade_early`: an early disengager and/or an early "just tell me" asker is moved from SOCRATIC/FLUENCY to FADED (a worked partial example) after 2 (both: 1) questioning turns without progress instead of 3 (Appendix D §3.3: "a disengaging one gets moved to telling sooner");
  - `scaffolded_explanation`: the C.14 sentence stem;
  - `seeded_declines`: an adaptation turned down across sessions and (almost) never taken is not offered again unprompted (cross-session task agreement);
  - `idle_nudge_paced`: the idle nudge never comes before 1.5 × the learner's typical reply time (capped at 60 s).
- **Boundaries.** A session that ended on a safety stop, a consent revocation or an error updates only its persona-rapport session count (so the next persona is not falsely "new"): the profile never becomes a secondary record of a disclosure. A failed profile read is never treated as an empty profile: nothing is written over real history and the context carries no guess. Numbers and closed labels only.
- **Interpretable, resettable, retained.** `GET /api/v1/tutor/disposition` (the learner's own) and `GET /api/v1/tutor/kids/:kidUserId/disposition` (a verified Tutor) return the plain-word explanation (`explainProfile`), rendered by the rebuilt `DispositionSummary`. Reset follows the memory-review rule (OD-18): a child's profile is reset by their verified Tutor (`DELETE /api/v1/tutor/kids/:kidUserId/disposition`, audited); a teen without a guardian link and an adult reset their own (`DELETE /api/v1/tutor/disposition`); a child cannot. Deleted with the account; not current after 30 days; purged after 365 days without an update by `npm --prefix backend run tutor:alliance-report -- --purge-stale`.

## 5. Kill switches (Appendix F Part 3 Stage 7)

| Component | Manual switch | Automatic rollback (Core) | Rollback action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alliance Controller | `TUTOR_ALLIANCE_CONTROLLER=act\|shadow\|off` (unknown → `act`) | A persona's bond proxy more than 15% below its own baseline (baseline: answers 14–90 days old, at least 50; current: the last 14 days, at least 30), or the latest 50 renegotiations with a known result improving fewer than half of the sessions. Written to `audit_logs` (`mentor.kill_switch.alliance.triggered`, personas and numbers only); holds until `tutor:alliance-report -- --resolve="…"`. A failed read never trips it | `shadow`: the renegotiation trigger and the continuity re-establishment are suspended (recorded as `shadow`); goal agreement and the bond/goal tracking keep running. Oracle applies the stricter of the switch and Core's verdict |
| Self-explanation move | `TUTOR_SELF_EXPLANATION=act\|shadow\|off` | none (the SPEC names no Stage 7 condition for C.14; the Pass Rate is diagnostic) | `shadow`: decision points recorded, never prompted |
| Disposition effects | none of its own | none | The effects follow the profile Core sends; a reset or a failed read sends none |

## 6. Instrumentation, metrics and tests (Appendix F)

- Close record (strict bodies; an emotion label, learner text or inconsistent counts is a 400 that closes nothing): `alliance`, `selfExplanation`, `disposition`.
- `npm --prefix backend run tutor:alliance-report`: Goal-Agreement Completion Rate (sessions with at least three learner turns; below 95% with 50 sessions it is a defect, exit 1), continuity moves, Adaptation-Offer Renegotiation Trigger Rate and the improvement split, Alliance Bond Proxy Score per persona with the specific-praise share, Self-Explanation Quality-Check Pass Rate per concept family, Disposition-Profile Completeness Rate, and the Alliance Controller's Kill-Switch Trigger Log.
- Stage 2 simulated students (`oracle/src/tutor/allianceGym.ts`, in `npm run gym:pedagogy`): repeated decliner, reactant teen, persona switcher (DoD c), filler explainer, concept explainer, frustrated, masking, steady — each proven able to turn red against a deliberately broken controller.
- `npm run alliance:check` (repo gate in `repo-gates.yml`): Oracle, Core, the migration and the client agree on every vocabulary, report field, projection field, bond-proxy answer and goal frame.
- Bias audit: `self_explanation.quality`, `self_explanation.decision_answer` and `alliance.goal_reply` are registered with paired regional, vernacular, code-switched, child-spelled and speech-to-text fixtures in three locales.

## 8. What reaches the model, stated plainly

Nothing new about the learner. The model receives: the goal instruction (which asks it to use words already in the transcript), the renegotiation and self-explanation instructions (fixed system text), the concept wording of the follow-up (our catalogue text), and, for a first meeting / persona switch / memory gap, one fixed continuity sentence. That sentence is derived server-side from the learner's history (it says, in effect, "this persona has not worked with this learner"); it is recorded in §9 as a proposal for the owner's reviewed decision on the context allow-list.

## 9. Proposals recorded for owner and pedagogy review

1. **The continuity sentence** travels as a fixed system instruction, not as a context field; confirm that a closed, server-derived instruction of this kind is outside the 14-field allow-list (otherwise the alternative is to filter `previousSessions` for a persona switch, which loses useful history).
2. **Persisting adaptation declines** as decayed counts, and seeding the plan with persistently declined adaptations, departs from the legacy rule in `plan.ts` that a decline is never persisted ("not a permanent label"). The SPEC's C.7/C.15 make declines alliance data in a persistent profile; the counts decay, are guardian-visible and resettable. Confirm.
3. **The bond proxy is asked after the close** on the closing surface (three chips), not spoken as another Mentor turn, so the recap is not lengthened. Confirm, and confirm "A little" as the middle answer.
4. **Goal agreement in shadow mode keeps running** (the Stage 7 table names only renegotiation and continuity as suspended). Confirm.
5. **Every threshold** in the log (decline pattern 2, window 4, 30-day memory gap, 95% reading of "near 100%", the renegotiation improvement floor 50%, the trait thresholds, the 365-day retention) is proposed, pending calibration; the 365-day retention needs legal review with the rest of the data-retention schedule.
6. **Stage composition** of the goal chips and the bond proxy, and the Family Hub placement of the profile, are wave-2 work; the 84 new clips (6 new texts plus the reworded re-engagement line, × 4 characters × 3 locales) need the owner's one-time synthesis approval under OD-23.

## 10. Limitations and open items

No physical PostgreSQL has applied the migration and the database types are not regenerated (Core uses narrow local types). No live or canary conversation has exercised any move (OD-23). No production data exists, so Appendix F's "Measured" criterion (the Bond Proxy Score live and segmentable by persona within one release cycle) is open. The Stage 2 personas are a regression floor, not a validation. The explanation-quality lexicon and the goal-reply reader need native-speaker review of their fixtures. Tier 1 sign-off is pending.
