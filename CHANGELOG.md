## Unreleased

### Added

- The avatar test page at `/dev/avatars` (dev server only) now has every control in one place: characters, 19 expressions, 12 gestures, 13 mouth shapes, look, text in three languages, an audio file for lip sync, and a slider for every rig number; `Avatar` gained an `overrides` prop for it.

- Character avatars (D-015, T-022, T-023): the four characters as layered 2D vector busts with named parts (`frontend/src/features/avatars/assets`, 4 to 13 KB each), an own rig and a React `Avatar` and `FloatingAvatar` that blink, breathe, follow the pointer, turn the head by parallax, hold six expressions and move the mouth when speaking (text-based visemes until the generated voice exists), a development page at `/dev/avatars` that is not in the production build, and then a much more expressive rig: per-side brows, lids, pupils, nose wrinkle, a bendable mouth, cheeks, head turn, tilt, nod, lean and bounce on springs, 19 expressions and 12 gestures. See [[CHARACTER-AVATARS]].
- Avatar tools in `frontend/tools/avatars/` (reference renders of the four heads, a numbered piece map, a Bezier fitter, art specs per character and a builder that writes layered named SVG) and the first clean drawings of Rho, Zara, Liruf and Dina in `frontend/tools/avatars/out/`, 5 to 11 KB each, in [[CHARACTER-AVATARS]]. Second pass: busts down to the shoulders for all four, organic outlines, tonal shading, clipped blush. Review fixes: no green patch between Liruf's eyes, Dina reduced to a neck that widens downward, Liruf's outline as single smooth shapes and a snout drawn as a volume, a rule to check the turned 3D views (`avatars:reference --yaw`), soft volume shading for all four, and a vivid iris for Liruf. See [[CHARACTER-AVATARS]].
- [[CHARACTER-AVATARS]] (draft): a researched plan for light 2D vector heads of the four characters inside the platform: the owner's decisions (no 3D, no mouth cards), how to rebuild a head faithfully from the models, licenses checked, format candidates, spikes, risks, open questions. ### Changed

- The platform is for ages 12 to 18 (D-013): the landing, its search description and the first skill now say 12 instead of 6, in the three languages, and the legal inputs are recorded in [[CHILD-SAFETY-AND-PRIVACY]]. The range is final; how 12-year-olds sign up is T-020.
- The frontend moved to React 19, Tailwind CSS v4 and shadcn. The hand-written design system (`src/rebuild/`) and its asset manifest were deleted; the landing, shell, cookie choice, theme toggle and language menu were rebuilt on the libraries, with brand tokens in `src/styles/lf-theme.css`. The "ten seeds" row became a Bklit ring chart. Public assets moved from `/rebuild/...` to `/landing/` and `/brand/`; copy files from `rebuild-*.json` to `core.json` and `site.json`.
- Landing copy rebuilt (see [[LANDING-MESSAGING]]): a headline that speaks to the parent ("Let your kids practice money before it's real"), the four skills as a numbered list, two statements about what stays true, and a closing call to action, in en-US, es-MX and pt-BR, with a shared type scale and a fixed 16 px floor. The 3D compositions are unchanged. The search title, description and prerendered HTML follow the new copy, and no longer promise a free start or an AI Mentor.
- Landing laid out on one 4, 8 and 12 column grid (rule 2 in [[FRONTEND-GUIDE]]): one gutter, one left edge for every text block, the same space between all sections, hero and problem text and image on six columns each and centered on each other (they stacked, text first, below 1024 px), the closing call to action left aligned on the grid, and the header aligned with the content on phones. Measured before and after at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.
- Landing: phones are centered and symmetric (hero, steps, principles, closing ask and footer); the problem section is a mirrored composition at every width, with its scene cropped so the characters sit on the same horizontal line as the text (they were 37 % of the frame too low); running text is medium weight in the soft ink color and 17 px on phones.
- The frontend audit tools in `frontend/tools/audit/` (D-010): accessibility with axe-core, keyboard, layout overflow, grid measuring, scene framing, color contrast and color-vision separation, and full-page screenshots, run by hand with `npm run audit:*`, with a `--matrix` option for every width and language. Developer tools, not a CI gate. `axe-core` is a new development dependency. The first run of the keyboard tool found the footer logo link 36 px tall (now 48 px).
- [[FRONTEND-TOOLKIT]] and a complete [[FRONTEND-GUIDE]]: the stack and commands, the folder structure, conventions, the accessibility floor, current performance, how a change is checked, the open decisions, which skill or library to use for each job, the repeating procedures, the known traps and how each mechanical check is run. Stale statements corrected (React 18 in the architecture, the 72 px target in UI-LIBRARIES) and the environment, testing, style and performance documents now point to them.
- Footer rebuilt in black with white text in every theme, with the logo in white and four titled columns (About us, Product, Behind the scenes, Follow us), the social marks (no names shown, no links until their addresses are set), and the terms, privacy notice, cookie preferences, contact address and copyright in small type at the bottom, on the page grid. The columns have no links under them yet.
- Sileo toasts take the brand colors and show their text as written; Sileo shares our Motion copy (`overrides` in `package.json`), which cut the entry chunk from 987 KB to 860 KB.
- Landing brought to the five frontend rules: brand name, contact address and language names moved into the locale files, the loading orb is named in the active language, no overflow from 320 to 1440 px in the three languages, a clear single-column grid and full-width actions on phones, buttons that wrap instead of cutting a label, and the young-child touch target token set to 76 px (2 cm).
- Accessibility pass on the landing (axe, keyboard): the skip link is the first Tab stop, targets are at least 48 px, closing the cookie sheet returns focus to its opener, and menus no longer contain a focusable scroll wrapper (one local patch to copied code, listed in [[UI-LIBRARIES]]). Stable libraries are built into their own chunks.
- `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` is off and registry code is excluded from ESLint and `lint:slop`, because the libraries' copied code does not satisfy them.
- `frontend-ci.yml` no longer triggers on the removed branch and paths. `frontend-cd.yml` now fetches the LFS 3D scenes (about 6 MB) before building, so a deploy does not ship pointer files.
- The product was restarted from zero (2026-10-09). Only the landing page, the 3D characters, the pinned Supabase setup and Pulse remain; see [[DECISIONS]].
